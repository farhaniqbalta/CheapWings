## Checkpoint C — Major Airports

Major is opt-in independently for origin and destination. MVP supports one parent
city/country per input; selecting a new parent explicitly replaces the prior
Major selection instead of mixing scopes. Exact sorted codes, names, count,
rationale and "Major airports only — not all airports in this location" appear
before submission. Unmapped locations clear Major scope and announce the manual
Specific alternative; no guessed subset or all-airport fallback exists.

Mode switch: Specific → Major with existing chips requires an inline explicit
clear action, with a Keep Specific option. Major → Specific retains every resolved
airport and removes Major metadata so the user can remove chips manually. Major
chips cannot be individually edited while still labeled Major. This avoids
misrepresenting an edited subset as the complete curated mapping.

The expanded production browser checkpoint passed 75 assertions at 375, 768 and
1440 pixels: deterministic TYO list, canonical submission metadata, missing US
mapping, retained chips on cancel/switch, and full Japan list even over budget.

### Known limitations and owner decisions

- Owner reviews the initial ten editorial mapping lists/rationales and the one-
  parent-per-side Major UX before acceptance. No further product decision is
  required to exercise the implemented MVP; publishing and merging remain gated.
- Major mappings deliberately cover only five countries and five metros. Other
  locations are browsable in Specific mode and explicitly unmapped in Major.
  Adding coverage needs reviewed curation and a catalog version bump.

  API traffic; normal static compression/caching may be configured at deployment.
  No service worker or guaranteed first-visit offline installation is added.

  are unknown metadata. No catalog claims complete commercial coverage.
- Automated keyboard/ARIA and touch-emulation checks plus screenshot inspection

- Existing swoop live transport failure remains a separate P4.9 follow-up.

  formatting cleanup is included. Existing web hooks/App warnings remain.
