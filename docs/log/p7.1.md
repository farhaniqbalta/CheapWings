**Desk outcome:**
- **Fli and swoop fail** the fare-first one-way gate: one is unpriced or roundtrip-only, and Fli has no Explore at all.

## Exact-date bridge (Skiplagged)

`sk_destinations_anywhere` probably returns a `dateRange` description, not a date.

Skiplagged also has `sk_flex_departure_calendar` [SK-rec]. For one route around a chosen date, it returns `points[].{departure, price, usdPrice}`. Its description says "the lowest one-way fares around a chosen departure, returning date → cheapest-price entries". That turns a destination lead into exact dated prices.

So discovery on Skiplagged would take two call kinds:
1. One anywhere call per origin per anchor date.
2. One flex-calendar call per shortlisted destination.

Both are bounded by the discovery call budget. The `select_discovery_plan` contract (Task 4) is unaffected: flex points become dated `DiscoveryObservation`s, and anywhere rows without a date are counted as undated.

The probe has to show whether `dateRange` holds a usable exact date, and how many days the flex calendar covers.

## Probe request (needs owner approval)

**Skiplagged only.** Fli and swoop are ruled out by desk research. Travelpayouts is not probed without a separate owner decision on the affiliate account and token.

| Call | Count | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tools/list` | 1 | Confirm the tool names and schemas still match the 2026-11-09 recording |
| `sk_destinations_anywhere`, one-way, `currency=IDR`, CGK / SUB / DPS × anchor dates 2026-12-22 and 2027-01-26 | 6 | Destinations, prices and `dateRange` content per origin and window |
| `sk_flex_departure_calendar` for the 2 cheapest destinations from each origin, first window only | 6 | Exact dates, days covered, price agreement with the anywhere price |
| `sk_flights_search` for the cheapest flex date of 1 destination per origin | 3 | Whether a detailed search finds a comparable fare (verification yield) |
| **Total** | **16** | Run one at a time, at least 60 s apart, so about 16 min. Stop at the first 429/401/403. No retries. |

**Recorded per call:**
- valid priced rows and unique destinations
- whether exact dates are present
- one-way semantics
- currency, `rateAsOf`, and any price age
- hidden-city indication
- latency and response size
- errors

Plus reproducibility notes, and how the anywhere price compares with the flex and detailed prices.

Fixtures are redacted as in P0: deep-link tracking parameters are stripped and no identifiers are kept.

**Go criterion (plan Task 3):** priced rows with exact departure dates, through `dateRange` or the flex calendar, for at least two of the three origins.
