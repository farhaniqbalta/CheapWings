# P7.2 / P7.3: Discover contract and shared Rust selection

Branch `p7.2-discovery-core` → `p7-implementation` (PR #26). Plan: [p7-plan](p7-plan.md) Tasks 4–7. Owner GO on providers: [P7.1](p7.1.md).

## What exists now
- **Contract (P7.2):** `core/cheapwings-core/src/discovery.rs` holds `DiscoveryObservation`, `FxRate`, `DiscoveryQuery`, `SelectedLead`, `DiscoveryCounts`, `DiscoveryPlan` and `DiscoveryError`. The hand-specified fixture `contracts/fixtures/core/discovery_fixtures.json` (16 cases, prices fictitious) is the source of truth.
- **Selection (P7.3):** `select_discovery_plan(observations, query, fx, max_route_dates)`.
  - It validates the query, reusing Search's `normalize`/`parse_date`.
  - Each observation is counted under the first rule it fails: invalid → hidden_city → undated → out_of_window → no_fx → over_budget (budget × 1.10).
  - FX rates must be positive (else `no_fx`), and a price that rounds to zero or below in the display currency counts as `invalid` (final-review fix).
  - Of what survives, it keeps the cheapest lead per destination, sorts by comparable price then destination, and applies the cap.
- **Bindings:**
  - iPad: UniFFI `selectDiscoveryPlan(...)` throws `DiscoveryPlanError.Rejected{code, detail}`.
  - Web: PyO3 `cheapwings_core.select_discovery_plan(payload: str) -> str`, JSON in and out through `discovery::web_payload`. Errors raise `ValueError("{code}: …")`, and malformed JSON gives `invalid_payload`.
- Search is unchanged: `plan_search` and its fixtures pass as before.

**Same fixture, identical output through Rust, UniFFI and PyO3.**

## Owner decisions (Task 4 gate, 2026-11-21)
- `BUDGET_HEADROOM = 1.10`: discovery may shortlist leads up to 10% over budget. Only detailed fares are "checked within budget".
- Undated observations are excluded and counted as `undated`, not spread across the window.
